自然 · Band 6.5约 8 分钟

The quiet power of
urban forests

城市里的树木不只是风景。越来越多的研究发现，它们正在悄悄改变我们的注意力、情绪与社区关系。

On a summer afternoon, a pocket of woodland can feel like a different city. The traffic is still there, but it becomes a distant texture rather than the main event.

Researchers have begun to describe this effect as soft fascination: a kind of attention that holds us gently, without demanding that we solve a problem or make a decision.

That may explain why urban forests matter beyond their environmental benefits. They give people a place to recover their attention, and sometimes, to notice one another again.
